# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

We are committed to providing a welcoming, respectful, and harassment-free environment for everyone who participates in the BridgAI community.

## Expected behavior

Participants should communicate respectfully, focus criticism on ideas and code, welcome different experience levels, accept technical feedback professionally, protect private information, and respect maintainers' review decisions.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, intimidation, personal attacks, discriminatory comments, publishing private information, deliberate disruption, and sharing malicious code without a responsible security context are not acceptable.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit, hide, reject, or remove content and contributions that violate this policy. Repeated or serious violations may result in temporary or permanent exclusion from project spaces.

Security research must follow [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).

## Scope

This policy applies in repository discussions, issues, pull requests, reviews, and other official BridgAI community spaces.
